The whole world is in debt. $348,000,000,000,000. 🌍

But debt has to be owed TO someone. So who does the entire planet actually owe?

The answer is a piece of paper called a bond — an IOU governments sell to borrow. And the biggest buyers aren't shadowy outsiders. They're pension funds, banks, and everyday savers. You, basically. The world mostly owes… itself.

The catch? The US now pays more in interest ALONE (~$970B/yr) than it spends on its entire military. That's not shrinking the debt. That's just renting it.
