Assalamu alaykum. Karim again. Every chart on this page comes down to three numbers. Learn them once... and you can read every post here.
Take a company. Call it Noor Incorporated. The market sells one share for fifty dollars.
Number one: the model's read. A fundamental-value model looks at the whole business — profits, growth, debt — and estimates a share is worth about eighty dollars. Fifty against eighty: the stock trades about thirty-seven percent below the model. That's a read... not a call.
Number two: the P E ratio. Noor earns two dollars fifty of profit per share each year. Fifty divided by two fifty is twenty. You're paying for twenty years of today's profit. Lower means cheaper... if the business holds.
Number three: growth. Noor's revenue grew fifteen percent this year. A marked-down company that's still growing... is a very different story from one that's shrinking.
Price versus the model. Years of profit. Growth. That's the toolbox behind every AI and quantum post here — and every stock still walks through the halal screen first.
A model's read is not a recommendation. Educational, not financial or religious advice.
