# Scripting Mechanics — Beat Sheet, Pacing, Captions, CTAs (2025-26)

Grounded for **@valuewithvalues**: flat-2D animated deadpan finance sketch, ~15-20s, captioned, optional TTS VO + newsroom bed.

---

## The beat sheet (template)

| Beat | Time window | Length | Purpose | Script rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 0:00-0:02 | ~2s | Scroll-stop; cold open on claim/visual, no lead-in | Payoff or promise of payoff visible before frame 1 finishes; no "hey guys" |
| **Setup** | 0:02-0:06 | ~4s | One sentence of context — what the viewer assumes / the situation | Max 1-2 short sentences; keep numbers concrete |
| **Turn** | 0:06-0:12 | ~6s | The mechanism/math/reveal — this IS the payoff | Front-load the number/chart; this is where the pattern interrupt lands |
| **Payoff/Button** | 0:12-0:16 | ~4s | Deadpan punchline or the "so what" | Land flat, let the visual carry emphasis (matches deadpan tone) |
| **CTA** | 0:16-0:20 | ~2-3s | One earned ask | Content-matched only — see CTA table below |

For 45-90s "Part 2" or comparison/walkthrough content, insert a **second hook** at 20-30s (new visual + fresh verbal line: "But here's the part nobody's pricing in...") before continuing to Turn/Payoff.

---

## Length & pacing

| Content type | Target length | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Single-insight / single-ticker take (our default) | **15-20s** (fits inside the tested 21-34s "sweet spot" band) | One hook, one payoff, one CTA |
| Real comparison / walkthrough (valuation, two-stock compare) | **45-90s** | Requires a planted 20-30s re-hook |
| Dense/multi-part (DCF, full fundamentals breakdown) | Split into **Part 1/2 at 15-20s each** | Cliffhanger cut at end of Part 1 |

**Don't optimize for shortest possible runtime.** The algorithm scores completion/rewatch %, not duration — a needlessly truncated video that cuts the real payoff underperforms a slightly longer one watched to the end.

### Words per second / speaking pace

| Register | WPM | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Relaxed | ~120 wpm | Rarely — reads too slow for finance short-form |
| **Standard explainer (our default VO pace)** | **135-160 wpm** | Core script — keeps tickers/numbers intelligible |
| Hype/reaction beat | 170-200 wpm | Layered accents only (e.g. a punch line), not core explanation |

Quick math for scripting: at 145 wpm ≈ **2.4 words/second**. A 20s video ≈ 48 words total VO — budget beats accordingly (Hook ~6-8 words, Setup ~12-15, Turn ~15-18, Button ~8-10, CTA ~5-6).

---

## Pattern interrupts

- **Cadence:** one real interrupt (cut, zoom, SFX hit, text-slam, visual-contrast beat) every **3-5 seconds**. Fewer than 3 in a 15-20s video feels flat; more than ~5 total reads chaotic and undercuts comprehension of numbers.
- **Audit method:** scan the timeline for any 5-second span with zero change (no cut/zoom/on-screen-text/sound cue) and plug it.
- **For our sketch format:** a scene/pose change, a chart zoom-punch, or a sting SFX on the number reveal all count. The Turn beat (0:06-0:12) is the natural home for the biggest interrupt — that's where the number/chart lands.

---

## Caption rules

- **Word-by-word (karaoke/highlight) captions, not static sentence blocks.** Each word pops/scales/changes color in sync with VO, ~250-400ms per word reveal. This is the dominant 2025-26 caption style and measurably beats static blocks on completion, engagement, and share rate.
- Captions carry retention independent of VO — **85%+ of social video is watched sound-off**; captioned Reels see 40-60% higher average watch time.
- Style captions, don't auto-dump them: timed, high-contrast, positioned to not fight the frame-1 hook overlay.
- Frame 1 still needs its own baked-in overlay text stating the value prop (this doubles as the "thumbnail" even before word-by-word caption kicks in on VO line 1).

## CTAs — converts vs cringe

| CTA | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Follow for Part 2" (on an episodic/cliffhanger video) | CONVERTS | Earned by content structure; platform already primed to serve ep. 2 |
| "Save this" (on a chart/data-dense video) | CONVERTS | Content-matched; saves are a strong ranking signal, especially finance infographic-style |
| Genuine debate prompt tied to the take ("agree or is this wrong?") | CONVERTS | Earned by an opinionated take; drives real comments, not bait |
| "Comment [WORD] and I'll DM you the [thing]" | CRINGE / THROTTLED | Explicitly detected and down-ranked by TikTok/IG engagement-bait systems in 2025-26 |
| "Comment YES if you agree" | CRINGE / THROTTLED | Same — named engagement-bait pattern, actively suppressed |
| Generic "follow for more" with no content link | CRINGE | Not earned by the specific video; low conversion |
| Stacking 2+ CTAs at the end | CRINGE | Dilutes the one clear ask; use exactly one |

**Rule:** exactly one CTA per video, chosen by what the content actually earned (episodic → follow; data-dense → save; opinionated → debate).

---

## Compliance line-item (finance-specific — fold into script, not an afterthought)

- Put "educational only, not financial advice" **on-screen and spoken within the first ~30 seconds** of any video that discusses a specific ticker/trade, restate later if the video runs past 30s, and restate in the caption. End-card-only disclosure is flagged as a top regulatory failure mode (SEC Dec-2025 risk alert).
- For our 15-20s sketch format, this typically means: a small persistent lower-third disclaimer for ticker-specific videos, spoken once in the button/CTA beat if the video is a trade-adjacent take (not needed for pure concept-education content).
