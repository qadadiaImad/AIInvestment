# Voiceover & Audio — Human vs AI/TTS, Delivery, Music (2025-26)

Grounded for **@valuewithvalues**: flat-2D animated deadpan finance sketch, ~15-20s, captioned, optional TTS VO + newsroom bed.

---

## Human vs AI/TTS — the core tradeoff

| Factor | Human / cloned voice | Stock AI/TTS voice |
|---|---|---|
| Audience trust | ~55% trust human voice | ~23% trust AI-generated voice — less than half |
| Production speed | Slower (record/direct) | Fast, scriptable, consistent |
| Fatigue risk | Low — distinctive, builds parasocial recognition | High if using a default/top-10 trending preset — viewers ID and dismiss in ~2 words |
| Best use here | Recurring "anchor" character voice across the channel | Fine for AI-production speed IF voice is non-default + emotion-tagged |

**Recommendation for our format:** since the sketch already has a deadpan animated newsroom-anchor character, a **cloned or consistent recurring TTS voice** (not a stock default) functions as that character's "voice" — treat it as a character asset, not a generic narrator. If using ElevenLabs/Play.ht, pick from the wider library (240+ voices), never the homepage default.

---

## Viral vs cringe voices

| Voice choice | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Obscure/non-top-10 ElevenLabs voice + explicit pacing prompt ("casual TikTok energy, micro-pause before payoff") | VIRAL | Avoids the "AI slop" instant-recognition tell while keeping AI production speed |
| Voice-cloned creator/character voice (~60s sample) | VIRAL | Builds recurring recognition; preserves authenticity premium |
| Emotion-tagged delivery (energy spike at hook, modulation through body, punch on payoff) | VIRAL | Flat AI delivery is the #1 cited driver of swipe-away |
| Hormozi-style authoritative, high-tempo, low-filler cadence | VIRAL for finance/business specifically | Reads as expertise, keeps info density high, correlates with completion in the niche |
| Default/top-10 trending ElevenLabs preset (2025's "Adam/Brian"-style) | CRINGE | Instantly recognized as generic AI-slop by 2026; viewers swipe within 2 words |
| Old robotic TikTok TTS ("Jessie"/Google-TTS sing-song) used unironically for serious content | CRINGE | Reads as a stale multi-year meme, kills financial credibility |
| Generic cinematic "movie trailer" deep narrator voice | CRINGE | Wrong register for explainer/trust content; overproduced, insincere, heavily templated elsewhere |
| Flat, monotone, no emotional tagging | CRINGE | Single most-cited driver of AI-voice fatigue regardless of voice quality |

## Delivery checklist for our scripts

1. Script emotional variance explicitly before sending to TTS — mark it in the script itself: `[energy: high]` on the hook line, `[energy: conversational]` through setup/turn, `[energy: flat/deadpan punch]` on the button line (deadpan tone still needs a *deliberate* flatness, not default monotone — direct the flatness).
2. Default pace: **135-160 wpm** (see scripting.md) — Hormozi cadence means short punchy sentences and minimal filler, not necessarily faster speech.
3. Design audio-first: write the VO line and its rhythm before locking the storyboard/animation timing, so cuts land on vocal emphasis (word-slam syncs with a pose change or chart zoom).
4. Never treat captions as a checkbox — word-by-word styled captions are mandatory pairing regardless of VO quality (see scripting.md caption rules); most viewers are sound-off.

---

## When to go captions-only (no VO)

- Rapid-fire numeric/chart content where the visual + caption carries the full payoff and a VO would just narrate what's on screen redundantly.
- Testing a new script fast/cheap before committing render time to full VO + animation sync.
- Not recommended as the default for @valuewithvalues — the deadpan anchor-character format leans on a consistent voice for recognition; reserve captions-only for occasional pure-data/infographic-style posts (which also independently save at ~3x the rate of talking-head finance content on Instagram).

## Music / audio bed strategy

| Practice | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **Newsroom bed (our default)** | Keep it low, ducked under VO during speech, swelling only in silent/pause beats (hook pop, button landing) — mirrors real newsroom mixing, reinforces the "financial anchor" bit |
| **Trending-sound strategy** | Native/trending audio is a discovery lever, but for a recurring branded format (deadpan newsroom bit), a **consistent bed/stinger** builds show-identity recognition — treat trending sound as an occasional variant, not the backbone |
| **Ducking** | VO must sit clearly above the bed at all times during speech (aim for bed ~ -15 to -20dB under VO); let the bed breathe up only in the 0.5-1s pause after the hook or before the button/punchline |
| **SFX as pattern interrupts** | Use sting/whoosh/text-slam SFX at the Turn beat's number reveal — doubles as both a pattern interrupt (scripting.md) and an audio cue that this is the payoff moment |
| **What's overused** | Generic corporate "breaking news" stock sting used with zero variation across every single video (recognizable-but-stale if it's literally the same 1-second clip every post — vary the stinger every few videos while keeping the "newsroom" register); meme audio grafted onto serious financial takes; Subway-Surfers-style split-attention audio/gameplay under finance VO (actively hurts credibility and comprehension, flagged as an antipattern) |

---

## Applying to our sketch format specifically

- The deadpan anchor character + newsroom bed is already a strong differentiated identity — protect it by keeping voice and bed **consistent across episodes** rather than chasing every trending sound.
- Emotion-tag scripts even though delivery is "deadpan" — deadpan is a *directed* flatness (calm, matter-of-fact) distinct from *undirected* monotone (robotic, lifeless); write `[deadpan]` as an explicit tag, not the absence of a tag.
- Reserve the 170-200 wpm hype register for a rare punch-line beat only, never the anchor's core delivery — it would break the deadpan bit.
