## Convergent findings (where advocates AND skeptics agree)

1. **Trust is the product.** Buyers judge in two courts at once — finance (competence) and fiqh
   (legitimacy/"selling the deen"). Every proven monetizer holds a face (PIF 71.8K YT, IFG 246K)
   or a scholar board (Musaffa, Zoya). We launch with neither → the #1 gap to close by design.
2. **The cold start is the binding constraint, not the product.** The 9k incumbent's growth was
   its 213K-follower Instagram, not its (dead) community. Our automated carousel/reel engine is
   the substitute weapon; expect slower ignition and a 4–8-week free "audit period" before anyone pays.
3. **The winning shape is a SEQUENCE, not a single bet** (psychology brief's bottom line):
   launch **A** → use **B-flavored content** (not a B community) as top-of-funnel hooks → add
   **H's fenced active track at ~500+ members** (marketed inside, never the headline) → open
   **C (French)** as a second community once the EN flywheel spins.

## Pros/cons verdicts

- **A** — bullseye on the payer persona; fiqh-safest; freshness-decay = retention mechanic; but
  double trust deficit, commoditized-from-below (Musaffa $6/mo) and out-faced-from-above (PIF),
  natural experiment warning (17 members @ $47/mo), weak virality. **Launch this, patched with
  B-flavored hooks.**
- **B** — verified-empty lane and the strongest identity hook ("permission to act"), but empty on
  Whop too (demand-desert signal), worst buyer economics, banned-retention-drip paradox, max
  backlash surface ("halal"+"trading"+anonymous+paid = scam template). **A content strategy, not
  a business. Use its language, not its brand.**
- **H** — full persona coverage and an activity engine, but at launch: incoherent positioning,
  unenforceable feed-fence, scam pattern-match, double content burden. **Correct as an evolution
  at ~500+ members; wrong as a launch.**
- **C** — genuine zero-competition + uncopyable native-FR moat + category-defining data product
  in French; but triple cold start, tens-of-payers ceiling arithmetic, worst regulatory
  jurisdiction (AMF/CIF + 2023 influencer law), FR scam-primed skepticism. **Ideal second
  community; wrong first move.**

## Trust architecture (non-negotiables regardless of concept)

Cite-never-rule ("passes the AAOIFI screen at X%", never "this is halal"); show timestamped math
including losses; religious fundamentals free forever — charge for labor (screens, portfolio
maintenance), never for rulings; zero trading-culture aesthetics; visible charity % of revenue;
annual pricing > monthly (frame-match to long-term discipline); founding-member framing ("first
100 lock $19/mo for life" — institution-building, sadaqah-jariyah-adjacent); skip trials — publish
last week's screen free, this week's paid (freshness decay does the selling).

## Compliance flags raised by the debate (resolve before launch)

1. **GuruFocus GF-Value cannot be redistributed in a paid tier** (gated provider data; our own
   rules + ToS). Paid screens must use AAOIFI ratios computed from primary/raw fundamentals; GF-Value
   stays internal or is replaced by our own valuation calc.
2. **A paid "model portfolio" edges toward regulated investment advice** — France-based creator ⇒
   AMF exposure even publishing in English; needs jurisdiction review + education-not-advice
   architecture before the paid tier opens.
3. **Uncertified screening loses public disagreements** with scholar-backed apps (Musaffa/Zoya).
   Mitigation: publish the full methodology, name the standard, show the math, and pursue a
   scholar review/advisor relationship as soon as revenue allows.
4. Creator facelessness is a live decision: this niche punishes anonymous finance brands; an
   on-camera presence or named scholar partnership materially changes every concept's odds.
