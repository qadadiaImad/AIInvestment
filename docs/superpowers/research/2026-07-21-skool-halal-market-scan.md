## 2. The decisive gaps (verified via Skool discovery `__NEXT_DATA__`, 15 queries)

1. **The halal TRADING lane is EMPTY.** "islamic trading" and "shariah trading" return **zero**
   results in Skool discovery. "halal forex" (5), "halal crypto" (3), "muslim trader" (2) — none
   are substantively halal. The only pairing of trading + halal label is `part-time-trader-6199`
   (163 members, halal in title only). None of the 6 largest generic trading groups (45k…3k)
   mention swap-free/Islamic accounts anywhere. ⚠️ Fiqh caution: this lane is partly empty
   because active trading is scholar-contested — any entry must be spot-only / no-margin /
   no-options and lead with methodology credibility.
2. **No data/tooling-driven halal community exists.** Every incumbent sells static course video +
   chat. Nobody ships live screens, fresh fundamentals, or model-portfolio dashboards inside
   Skool. (Off-Skool, PIF does model portfolios at $360–600/yr — the closest analogue.)
3. **FR/AR = totally empty on Skool.** Zero French halal-investing communities ("finance
   islamique", "bourse halal" → only English hits); French demand served off-Skool
   (mousliminvestor.com, halalpatrimoine.com, startup-muslim.com "+3000 membres"). Arabic Muslim
   groups are large (Halal Success ~15.7k across 2 groups) but generalist make-money-online, no
   AAOIFI stock content.
4. **Growth-vs-monetization structure is proven:** on Skool, *everything* >5k members is free;
   paid finance groups cluster **$49–149/mo** (median ~$69–99) at 50–2,000 members. The scale
   model = free community → paid backend.

## 5. Positioning prospects derived from the scan

- **A. "Halal growth-stock investing, powered by live data"** — free Skool community + paid tier
  (~$29–49/mo founding): simple procedures (broker → AAOIFI screen → buy spot → purify → zakat →
  rebalance) applied to high-conviction sectors (AI stack first), with weekly *fresh-data* screens
  (our engine: fundamentals, debt ratios for AAOIFI, GF-Value) — the differentiator no halal
  incumbent has. Fills gap #2; avoids fiqh controversy; carousels/reels engine = content funnel.
- **B. "Halal Trading, Simplified"** — claim the literally-empty halal-trading lane (gap #1):
  spot-only, cash-account, no-margin/no-options swing rules on screened stocks. First-mover
  keyword capture, but scholar-contested → must be methodology-first, signals-cringe-free,
  scholar-cited. Higher risk, higher differentiation.
- **C. Francophone "Investissement Halal"** — gap #3 blue ocean, zero Skool competition, owner is
  francophone; smaller pool, demand proven off-Skool. Strong phase-2 or parallel-funnel candidate.
