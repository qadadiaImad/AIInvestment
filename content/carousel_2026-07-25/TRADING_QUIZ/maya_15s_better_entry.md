# Maya — 15s: "DCA still has a price"

**Thesis.** Long-term investing and dollar-cost averaging are the right default.
That is not in question. The point is narrower: *within* that strategy, the price
you pay still compounds, and price is not random — it stalls at the same levels
repeatedly. Technical analysis is pattern recognition on that repetition. It does
not pick what to own; it says when not to rush.

Deliberately **not** claimed: that TA predicts direction, that DCA should be
paused, or that anyone should time the market. The claim is only that a better
entry is a free improvement on a decision you were already going to make.
