# TA quiz reels — captions

One entry per rendered reel. Each carries a primary (feed) caption, a short
(Story / second-slide) cut, and hook alternates for A/B.

House rails that apply to every caption here:

- The OHLC is **synthetic** unless the entry says otherwise — never attach a
  ticker or timeframe to it, never imply it is a real chart.
- Education, not calls. No entries, no targets, no "this will drop".
- Name the **invalidation**. The patterns in this set are the ones most often
  read backwards after the fact; a caption that only describes the winning case
  teaches a bias.

## #79 — Liquidity Sweep / Stop Hunt

**Primary**

> The level broke. Then it didn't. 🎣
>
> That wick below the swing low wasn't a breakdown — it was a **liquidity grab**.
>
> Here's why it happens. Everyone who bought that low put their stop just under it. Everyone waiting to short is watching for the break. That makes the space below an obvious low a pile of resting orders — free fuel for anyone big enough to need it. Price dips in, collects it, and leaves. 💸
>
> The tell is the **speed of the reclaim**: price wicks through the low and closes back above it *within the same candle*. Long lower wick, body back on the right side. A real breakdown doesn't get undone in one bar. ⚡
>
> And the rule cuts both ways — if it closes *below* and stays there, that's not a sweep, that's a breakdown, and you were wrong. The close is what tells you which one you got. ✅
>
> You didn't get stopped out by the market. You got stopped out by the crowd standing exactly where you were. 🧠
>
> Synthetic chart built to show the pattern — not real market data. Educational only, not financial advice. DYOR ⚠️
>

**Short**

> The level broke. Then it didn't. 🎣
>
> A wick below the swing low, reclaimed in the same candle — that's a liquidity grab, not a breakdown. Your stop was the fuel. 💸
>
> The tell is the close. Back above = sweep. Stays below = you were just wrong. ⚡

**Hooks**

**Note.** Kept the "if it closes below and stays there, you were wrong" line.
Every failed long gets relabelled a stop hunt after the fact; naming the
invalidation is what keeps this one honest.
