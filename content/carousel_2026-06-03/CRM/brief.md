## Full fundamental walkthrough

**Place in the stack:** Application / Software layer of the AI value chain (sector: Technology Services; industry: Packaged Software). For this layer the ratios that "explain" value are P/S, EV/Rev, revenue growth, gross margin and the Rule of 40 — multiples and margin quality, because the asset base is software, not fabs or power.

**The gap.** Price 2026-06-02 = **$200.84**. My modeled fundamental value = **$319.92** (margin of safety **~37–40%**, "significantly undervalued" on this framework). The market is pricing CRM as a slowing, ex-growth name; the fundamentals look better than that.

**The dominant factor — operating margin.** CRM runs a **~21.9% operating margin** against a Packaged-Software median of **~7.3%** — roughly 3×. Net margin **18.7%** vs ~5.2% median tells the same story. This is the single biggest reason the fundamental value sits well above price: the business converts revenue into profit at a rate most peers can't touch, and value models reward durable profitability.

**The supporting ratios:**
- **P/S 4.08×** vs industry median **3.61×** — modestly richer on sales, justified by the margin profile.
- **Gross margin 75.1%** vs **63.7%** median — premium software economics.
- **Revenue growth (YoY) 11.0%** vs **13.4%** median — the one place CRM trails; this is the bear's anchor.
- **Rule of 40 ≈ 45%** (11.0% growth + 34.2% FCF margin) — clears the 40 bar on cash alone.
- **FCF margin 34.2%** — about a third of revenue lands as free cash.
- **ROIC 10.9%** vs **4.5%** median — capital is working harder than the peer set.
- **P/E 23.2×** vs **25.5×** median — a market-average earnings multiple while EPS grew **35.1%** YoY.

**Synthesis.** A high-margin, cash-generative franchise growing a bit slower than peers, trading at an average P/E with above-average returns on capital. The slower top line is real and is why I respect the bear case (and the DA Davidson PT cut). But on margins, cash and returns, the franchise quality is what supports a fundamental value above today's price.

**Caveats / what decays:** "Fundamental value" is one analyst's modeled estimate — not a target, not a guarantee; models can be wrong and price can stay below value indefinitely. All figures are TTM/point-in-time and were retrieved 2026-06-02/03. Some peer-median fields (P/S, growth, ROIC, P/E) are TradingView scanner medians across ~180–295 Packaged-Software names; margin medians across ~286–292 names.
