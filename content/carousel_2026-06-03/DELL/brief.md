## Full fundamental walkthrough (the spoken script)

**Where it sits in the stack.** Dell is an **AI-infrastructure / hardware** name (Electronic Technology → Computer Processing Hardware). For hardware, the value-explaining ratios are P/E, P/FCF, gross margin, ROIC, and revenue growth.

**The gap.** Live price is **$435.31** (close 2026-06-02). The creator's fundamental-value read is **$162.37** — implying the market price is **~168% above** that model's fair value (margin of safety ≈ −168%; the harvested _guru.json figure is −159.33% on a slightly earlier reference price). However you slice it, this screens as richly valued on a pure cash-flow model.

**Why a premium can be defensible — the dominant factor (subjective): ROIC.**
- **ROIC ≈ 38.5%** vs an industry median of **~8.1%** — roughly **5× peers.** A box business that converts invested capital this efficiently is unusual and is the single best justification for a premium multiple. This is the call-out factor.
- **Revenue growth ≈ 38.9% YoY** vs **~11.0%** median — the AI-server cycle is showing up in the top line.
- **P/E ≈ 34.6** vs **~25.0** median — Dell trades richer than the typical peer; the market is paying up for the growth + returns above.

**The honest catch (slide 4).**
- **Gross margin ≈ 20.9%**, essentially **in line** with the ~21% peer median — hardware economics, not software.
- **Net margin ≈ 6.3%**, **FCF margin ≈ 7.0%** — slim by design; lots of revenue, a thin slice survives to cash.
- But the **engine is real:** EPS grew **~+101% YoY**, FY26 (ended 2026-01-31) revenue **$113.5B** (up from $88.4B two years earlier), and FY26 free cash flow **$8.6B**. The premium multiple is the market betting that AI-server demand keeps that engine running; the thin margins are the reminder that Dell sits in the infrastructure layer, not the model/application layer.

**News (last slide).** Hottest = Morgan Stanley lifting its DELL price target +164% amid an AI-server-outlook surge that also lifted HPE/SMCI (reported, 2026-06-02). Supporting: the **Dell AI Factory with NVIDIA** co-development program (reported, GTC 2026) and the **Sovereign AI EMEA** data-center network with **Palantir** on software (reported, 2026-01-21).

**Congress (public record).** Largest on-file DELL House disclosure: **Rep. Jared Moskowitz (FL-23), buy, $15,001–$50,000, txn 2025-10-10, filed 2025-11-22.** Several other members (Bresnahan, M.T.
