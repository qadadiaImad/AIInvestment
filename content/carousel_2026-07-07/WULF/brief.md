## Walkthrough (third person, pipeline-vs-statements)
TeraWulf trades at $20.38 after a +308% year, while the analysts' fundamental-value model pins the stock at $6.15 — the price sits roughly 231% above what the model will underwrite. That gap is not a verdict; it is two different clocks. The model reads trailing financials, and TeraWulf's trailing financials are deeply negative: a −611% net margin against a +12.2% peer median, a −2244% ROE against +16.1%, and no P/E at all because there are no trailing earnings to divide by (peers trade at a 39.5x median). The market, meanwhile, is pricing the reported $19 billion multi-year AI-compute lease with Anthropic — contracted future revenue the income statement has not seen a dollar of yet. The one trailing number already pointing that way is revenue growth: +27.3% year over year versus a +23.3% peer median. The bridge between the two clocks is execution: the capacity must be built, powered, and financed on time, and a loss-making balance sheet makes the financing terms part of the thesis. The next scheduled reality check is earnings on 2026-08-17, the first look at how — and how fast — the signed pipeline starts becoming recognized revenue. Until contracted revenue shows up in reported numbers, the price is carrying the story alone.
