On profitability, the honest frame is that the whole group is deeply lossmaking: the peer median net margin is −971.6%. The top factor: hypergrowth revenue against a sentiment washout — the gap is about the multiple resetting, not the trajectory. Because this is a story-priced, pre-profit group, the model's error bars are wide and re-rating risk cuts both ways: the same reflexive tape that took ~26% out in June can restore or extend the move just as fast. What to watch: bookings momentum, quantum-advantage proof points from the new initiatives, and the next earnings print expected around 2026-08-12.
