## Walkthrough (a relative read, not a quality call)

IonQ closed July 3 at $49.12 after a 26.1% June drawdown, and the analysts' model places fundamental value at $87.61 — a 43.9% discount, labeled "Undervalued." That label deserves heavy qualification: this is a relative read, not a quality call, because IonQ is pre-profit and the model's error bars are wide on story-priced names. Within its own quantum pure-play group, IonQ screens as the "cheapest" at ~97x sales versus roughly 468x (QUBT), 601x (RGTI) and 665x (QBTS), and it is the fastest grower at real scale, with revenue up +334.6% year over year to $130.0M. The unprofitability is severe and should not be softened: gross margin is −28.8%, operating margin −420.3%, and 2025 free cash flow was −$299.6M against a −$510.4M net loss. The balance sheet offsets some of that burn, with a 14.05 current ratio and near-zero debt (D/E 0.006). A 4.94 beta means the tape re-rates violently in both directions, so June-sized moves are a feature of the name, not an anomaly. The top factor is that revenue scaling is the one fundamental moving toward the story — the price itself remains narrative-led.
